# Privacy Policy

This application accesses Google Drive solely to write backup archive files
created by Mike McNees's own server, to his own Google Drive account, under
the `drive.file` scope (access limited to files this app creates).

- No data is shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.
- No data is used for advertising or analytics.
- The only user of this application is its owner, Mike McNees.
